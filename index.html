Football boots: choose the right pair for the right surface to ensure the best experience

Introduce

Football boots are a player's most important piece of equipment. A suitable pair of boots helps with good court grip, precise ball control, and injury prevention. Each type of playing surface requires a different type of shoe sole.

Common types of shoe soles.

  • FG (Firm Ground): for dry, firm natural grass pitches.
  • AG (Artificial Ground): for artificial turf.
  • TF (Turf): Low-profile studs, designed for short-pile artificial turf and small-sided pitches.
  • IC (Indoor Court): Flat sole, designed for indoor futsal courts.
